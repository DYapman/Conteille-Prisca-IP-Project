Passion, Sincerity, Humility

Watches & Wonders was founded in 2015 as a community between new followers of CP. Since then, the club has blossomed into a horological community; a modest fraternity of passionate people coming together to share their appreciation of beautiful timepieces.

We operate on three core values: Passion, Sincerity, and Humility. The doors of the club are always open to earnest and modest horology enthusiasts who seek more than just a status symbol.

The Inner Circle

Join a "wrist-check" tradition that signifies unity and shared identity. Membership offers you a seat at the table in a global network of collectors who understand that a watch is not just an accessory, but a conversation.

Beyond the Watch

Gain access to private events, exclusive releases, and technical workshops. Experience horology in a social, upscale setting where enthusiasts gather to examine rare calibers and discuss design philosophies.

Curated by Enthusiasts

Witness a diverse and high-end collection curated by the community, for the community. From vintage classics to modern avant-garde pieces, our society appreciates the full spectrum of horological art.
