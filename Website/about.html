ORIGINS

The history of Contielle Prisca began in 1967 founded by two friends, Darryl Yap and Marcus Valdez. Their partnership was a natural fit: from design to watchmaking, their passions fully aligned regardless of race.

One Indonesian and One Filipino, in the union of their respective skills, Contielle Prisca was born.

DESIGN

The clockwork gears was chosen as a logo to represent and idealize the vision Contielle Prisca has for watches: to embody "Defiant Elegance."

It represents a unique duality: the resilient, territorial strength of a steampunk paired with the fluid, ornamental beauty of a high-end timepiece.

ICONIC SIGNATURE

The Sea Mariner, the first watch produced by Contielle Prisca, bridges the gap between a rugged tactical instrument and a work of fluid art.

Engineered with the uncompromising utility of a classic "Sub" and the ornate, technical finishing of a "Seamaster," it features a 300m-rated case designed with a sleek, aggressive profile. It is a diver built for those who value the resilience of a warrior and the elegance of an icon.
